## Pages to Defer

| Page Idea | Why Defer |
|---|---|
| Every condition combination page | Risk of thin, repetitive SEO pages. |
| "Best app" pages in every planned locale | Competitor claims need native research and ongoing maintenance. |
| General "health tracker" pages | Too broad and pulls Zebra toward wellness framing. |
| Treatment-advice pages | Outside product scope and trust boundaries. |

## Content Standard

Every new page must answer a real patient job:

- What should I track?
- How does Zebra reduce appointment burden?
- What does Zebra not do?
- Can I use it on a bad day?
- How does this become useful proof for a doctor?
