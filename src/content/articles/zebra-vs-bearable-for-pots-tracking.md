Zebra and Bearable can both be used for POTS tracking, but they solve the problem differently. Bearable is a broad, highly customizable symptom tracker. Zebra is built more directly around orthostatic context, salt and hydration tracking, flare history, and doctor-ready appointment prep. If you want maximum customization, Bearable may fit better. If you want a faster path from POTS tracking to a clearer appointment summary, Zebra is the stronger fit.

For many people with POTS, the real question is not just “Which app can hold symptoms?” It is “Which app helps me understand upright symptom patterns and explain them later?”

## The biggest difference

Bearable starts from generic flexibility.
Zebra starts from POTS and dysautonomia-oriented structure.

That affects:

- how much setup you need
- whether orthostatic context stays connected
- how easy the history is to review before appointments
- whether the end goal is tracking alone or appointment preparation

## Where Bearable is strong for POTS

Bearable is strong if you want:

- a broad symptom tracker
- a customizable system you can shape yourself
- one app that is not tied to a specific condition pattern

That flexibility is useful for people who like building their own setup and do not mind more initial decisions.

## Where Zebra is stronger for POTS

Zebra is stronger if you want:

- orthostatic context beside symptom history
- salt, hydration, medications, and flares in one record
- symptom history that is easier to review before cardiology or specialist visits
- a doctor-ready report as the main payoff

If your main problem is “I tracked things, but I still have to rebuild the month from memory,” Zebra fits that gap more directly.

## Which one is better for doctor appointments?

If the point of tracking is to prepare for a doctor appointment, Zebra is usually the better fit.

That is because POTS appointments often require you to preserve:

- upright symptom changes
- flare history
- hydration and salt context
- medication changes
- questions for the visit

Those details are easier to use when they stay in one connected history.

## Which one is better for brain fog and bad days?

That depends on what makes tracking fail for you.

Bearable may still work well if:

- you like building your own system
- you can keep up with customization
- the main problem is not review

Zebra may work better if:

- the history keeps getting scattered
- you want fewer setup decisions
- the harder part is understanding the pattern later

If that is your main problem, [Why Symptom Tracking Fails on Bad Days](/blog/why-symptom-tracking-fails-on-bad-days/) explains the pattern directly.

## Bottom line

Bearable is a strong general tracker for POTS if you want flexibility first. Zebra is the stronger fit when you want POTS symptom history, orthostatic context, and appointment prep to stay connected from the start.
