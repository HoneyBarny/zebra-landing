If you need the best app for POTS cardiology appointment prep, the most useful choice is the one that helps you review symptoms, orthostatic context, medications, hydration, salt, and questions before the visit starts. If your real problem is scattered history, Zebra is the strongest fit. If you only need a temporary place to save numbers, simpler tools may still work.

The hard part of cardiology prep is rarely just collecting more data. It is making the data usable when the appointment actually begins.

## What makes a POTS cardiology prep app useful

The best app should help you answer questions like:

- What changed recently?
- What happens when I am upright?
- What did the bad days look like?
- What changed around medications, salt, or hydration?
- What do I want the cardiologist to understand first?

That is different from generic symptom tracking. Appointment prep needs review, not just capture.

## Notes, spreadsheets, and vitals-only tools

Simpler tools can still work if you only need short-term capture.

They may be enough if:

- the appointment is very soon
- you already know the main pattern
- you only need a few points to remember

The tradeoff is that POTS cardiology prep often depends on the relationship between:

- symptoms
- upright changes
- flare timing
- medications
- hydration and salt
- what changed in daily life

That is where disconnected tools start to break down.

## What should a POTS cardiology prep app include?

The most useful features usually include:

- symptom history
- orthostatic observations when relevant
- flare tracking
- medication and supplement notes
- hydration and salt context
- a question list
- a way to review the whole story before the visit

## What should you actually bring into the room?

The best app should help you walk in with:

- a short symptom summary
- the clearest orthostatic pattern notes
- medications and recent changes
- hydration and salt context if relevant
- your top questions

That is usually more useful than bringing a giant archive of raw entries.
