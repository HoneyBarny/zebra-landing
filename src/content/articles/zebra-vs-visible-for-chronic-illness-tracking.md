Zebra and Visible help with related chronic-illness problems, but they are not trying to do the same job. Visible is built around pacing and energy management with wearable support. Zebra is built around symptom history, orthostatic context, and doctor-ready appointment prep. If pacing is your main problem, Visible may fit better. If explaining what changed before an appointment is the harder problem, Zebra is the stronger fit.

That distinction matters because many people searching for a “symptom tracker” are actually trying to solve two different problems:

- pacing daily energy more carefully
- showing a specialist or cardiologist what changed over time

Those are connected, but they are not identical.

## The biggest difference

Visible is best understood as a pacing platform. According to Visible’s current product materials, it combines a wearable heart-rate monitor with an app to support pacing through features like a morning stability score, PacePoints, and pacing alerts. Zebra is better understood as a symptom-history and appointment-prep tool that keeps symptoms, flares, medications, orthostatic context, and notes in one record.

So the shortest version is:

- choose Visible if you want help pacing and understanding exertion
- choose Zebra if you want help organizing symptom history for appointments

## Where Visible is strong

Visible is strong if you want:

- pacing support as the main goal
- wearable-guided energy management
- day-to-day exertion alerts
- a tool focused on energy-limiting conditions

Visible’s own help materials emphasize pacing, energy envelopes, and continuous heart-rate support as core use cases. That makes it a good fit when the main question is, “How do I avoid overdoing it and crashing?”

## Where Zebra is stronger

Zebra is stronger if you want:

- one record for symptoms, flares, and appointment notes
- orthostatic context next to the rest of the day
- medications, salt, hydration, and function in the same history
- a doctor-ready report as the main payoff

Zebra is built for the point where scattered symptom history becomes hard to explain in a specialist visit.

If your main question is, “How do I stop rebuilding the month from memory before an appointment?” Zebra is the stronger fit.

## Which one is better for POTS and dysautonomia?

That depends on what you are trying to solve.

Visible can still be relevant for POTS or dysautonomia if pacing and exertion management are the main challenges. Its official materials also note that people with POTS and fibromyalgia use the platform, even though its strongest focus is on energy-limiting illness and pacing.

Zebra is the better fit if you are trying to keep:

- upright symptoms
- orthostatic observations
- symptom flares
- medication context
- salt and hydration changes
- questions for the visit

in one place for later review.

If the real problem is appointment reconstruction, Zebra fits that use case more directly.

If you describe the problem as autonomic symptoms rather than dysautonomia, [Best App for Autonomic Symptom Tracking](/blog/best-app-for-autonomic-symptom-tracking/) is the closer match.

## Which one is better for brain fog and bad days?

Visible may help if the hardest part is noticing when you are pushing beyond your limit.

Zebra may help more if the hardest part is remembering what happened later.

Those are different types of support:

- Visible helps with pacing decisions in the moment
- Zebra helps with symptom-history review before appointments

If you already know your bad days become hard to reconstruct, that usually points toward Zebra. If your main issue is staying inside a sustainable energy range, that usually points toward Visible.

## Do you need both?

Some people may end up using more than one tool because pacing and appointment preparation are different jobs.

But if your current setup already feels too fragmented, adding another tool only helps if it clearly solves the missing job. A good rule is:

- choose Visible if the missing job is pacing
- choose Zebra if the missing job is appointment prep

If your bigger problem is still “I have too many disconnected tools,” Zebra is often the better simplification step.

If you are preparing for a cardiology visit specifically, [Best App for POTS Cardiology Appointment Prep](/blog/best-app-for-pots-cardiology-appointment-prep/) and [Best App for Dysautonomia Cardiology Appointment Prep](/blog/best-app-for-dysautonomia-cardiology-appointment-prep/) narrow the choice around appointment outcomes.

## Bottom line

Visible is a better fit for pacing-first users who want wearable-guided energy management. Zebra is a better fit for people who need a clearer symptom history, orthostatic context, and a doctor-ready report before appointments.

The better choice is not about which app is “better” in general. It is about which problem is costing you more right now.
